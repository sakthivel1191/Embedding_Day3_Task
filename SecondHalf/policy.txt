
# Company Policy

## Leave Policy

Employees are eligible for  paid annual leave of 20 days.
Employees must apply for leave in advance.
Managers must approve leave requests.

## Attendance Policy

Employees must arrive at the office on time.
Working hours are from 9 AM to 6 PM.
Employees must inform their manager about late arrival.

## Work From Home Policy

Employees can work from home with manager approval.
Employees must attend all scheduled online meetings.
